About the history above. The long-term figures are calculated from broad market indexes matching each mix — total US stock market, global stocks outside the US, and the total US bond market — rebalanced once a year, with dividends reinvested. They are not the track records of the specific funds in your plan, several of which have shorter histories. Index history is used because it covers a much longer period and more market conditions. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results, and no allocation prevents loss.
